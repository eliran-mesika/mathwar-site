Privacy Policy

MathWar is in development. The current public site does not collect personal information, does not offer accounts, and does not include final App Store privacy labels for the unreleased game.

Development note: Final App Store privacy disclosures will be aligned with the shipped build before release. Until then, do not treat development notes as final privacy labels.

This static site does not include forms, cookies, account signup, or payment collection.

2. Game data

MathWar is not currently presented as a public App Store release on this site. Privacy disclosures for a released build will be reviewed against the actual shipped app before publication.

3. Accounts and payments

The current public site does not advertise accounts, multiplayer social features, subscriptions, or payments for MathWar.

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@mesikalabs.com. General MathWar support can be sent to support@mesikalabs.com.
