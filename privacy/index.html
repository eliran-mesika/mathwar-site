Privacy Policy

BrainMarshal is an arcade arithmetic runner from MesikaLabs. The game does not require an account and does not ask players to provide names, contact details, or payment information.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Site data

This static site does not include forms, cookies, account signup, or payment collection.

2. Game data and advertising

Game progress, settings, and language choices are stored locally on the device. MesikaLabs does not operate player accounts or a game backend for BrainMarshal.

Android version 1.0 (2): This testing build does not include an active advertising or analytics SDK and does not send game progress or player data to MesikaLabs. Ad-related reward screens are local simulations, not third-party ads. Progress and settings stay on the device; removing the app or clearing its storage removes this local data.

New Android releases with Google ads: The Android release candidate now includes Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform. These services process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or app identifiers for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. Google documents that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. The game does not request precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone access for advertising.

For every Android player, the game requests child-directed, non-personalized ads with a maximum content rating of G and the under-age-of-consent setting enabled. It disables the publisher first-party advertising identifier. Child-directed treatment instructs Google not to transmit the Android advertising identifier. Non-personalized advertising still involves the data processing described above; it does not mean that no data is collected. These Android settings do not change the separate iOS configuration described below.

Rewarded ads are optional: players can choose to watch one for a stated in-game benefit. The game also supports ads at level transitions. Advertising availability depends on the release, connection, region and provider. Local test builds use test ads. Android players can open Settings > Privacy Choices where Google makes additional choices available.

For Google's practices and controls, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. For questions about a child's information, a parent or guardian can contact privacy@mesikalabs.com. MesikaLabs does not operate a server copy of local game saves; deleting app storage removes local progress, but does not itself delete data already processed by Google.

iOS: BrainMarshal uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage advertising consent. Depending on the device, region, consent choices, and ad delivery, these services may collect device identifiers, coarse location, product interaction and advertising data, performance data, crash data, and other diagnostic data. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation.

On iOS, where required, the game presents a consent message before requesting ads. Players can revisit available choices from Settings > Privacy Choices. The current iOS build does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

3. Accounts and payments

BrainMarshal does not provide player accounts, multiplayer social features, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. MesikaLabs does not sell player-provided contact information because BrainMarshal does not collect player contact information.

4. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@mesikalabs.com. General BrainMarshal support can be sent to support@mesikalabs.com.
