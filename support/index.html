Current scope

The game is an arithmetic runner prototype for iPhone and iPad portrait. It focuses on math gates, squad growth and loss, readable mobile framing, and short level runs.

Known development boundary

MathWar is not a classroom curriculum, tutoring service, account platform, or multiplayer social product. It is a game-style arithmetic practice surface.
