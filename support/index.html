Development status

BrainMarshal is in development. Version 1.0 (1) is undergoing device testing; App Store review and TestFlight distribution are pending. When reporting a problem, include your device model, iOS version and the screen or action involved.

Game scope

BrainMarshal is an arithmetic runner for iPhone and iPad portrait. It focuses on math gates, squad growth and loss, readable mobile framing, and short level runs.

Known development boundary

BrainMarshal is not a classroom curriculum, tutoring service, account platform, or multiplayer social product. It is a game-style arithmetic practice surface.
