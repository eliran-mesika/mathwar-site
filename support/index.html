MathWar support

MathWar is currently in development. For support, feedback, TestFlight questions, or parent-facing questions, email support@mesikalabs.com.

Current scope

The game is an arithmetic runner prototype for iPhone and iPad portrait. It focuses on math gates, squad growth/loss, readable mobile framing, and short level runs.

Known development boundary

MathWar is not a classroom curriculum, tutoring service, account platform, or multiplayer social product. It is a game-style arithmetic practice surface.
