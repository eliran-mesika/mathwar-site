# MathWar Site Design Contract

- Keep this repo website-only. Do not modify the game source, prototype captures, or generated gameplay images from this site repo.
- Visual direction: playful tactical learning, with bright primary accents, readable math/game proof, and direct in-development language.
- Use the MathWar icon for favicon, Apple touch icon, brand marks, and compact navigation.
- Use web-sized icon and gameplay derivatives for public page delivery. Keep full-resolution source captures in the repo, but do not load them into compact UI slots.
- Navigation should expose App, Blog, Support, Privacy, and Terms where context allows. Homepage may also include Game and Learning anchors.
- Keep all public claims aligned with development state. MathWar is in development unless the main app repo and Apple state prove public release.
- Screenshots and gameplay captures should stay inspectable and clearly marked as prototype/current proof when relevant.
- Blog copy may be funny and direct, but must not promise curriculum replacement, tutoring, multiplayer, accounts, or App Store availability before those are true.
- Use `support@mesikalabs.com` for MathWar support until a dedicated address exists, `privacy@mesikalabs.com` for privacy, and `legal@mesikalabs.com` for legal.
